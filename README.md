One line — the funniest office joke, judged head to head:

> I replied-all that the meeting could have been an email; now there's a meeting about my email, and I'm chairing it.

The joke stacks two things anyone who has worked in an office knows at
once — the meeting that could have been an email, and the reply-all —
then turns a second time at the very end: the complainer is now chairing
the meeting about his own email. Setup, turn, punchline last, no wasted
words, and a fresh angle rather than a plain complaint about meetings.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure stdlib, deterministic, no network, no randomness) writes the
line to `/output/entry.txt`. Output: 115 characters, 21 words, 1 line, UTF-8,
within the 1–200 character gate.
